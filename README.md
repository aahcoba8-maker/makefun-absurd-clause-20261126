A make.fun entry for [The Absurd Clause Contest](https://make.fun/bounty/u-donrulezz-the-absurd-clause-contest-035cc0f1).

One sentence, at most 200 characters: an absurd clause that could hide deep inside a terms-of-service agreement. Deadpan legal boilerplate until the absurdity hits.

> Section 14.2: Any silence lasting longer than four seconds during a support call shall be construed as acceptance of all future price increases.

Run `python3 main.py` to write the entry to `entry.txt` (or to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` when set).
